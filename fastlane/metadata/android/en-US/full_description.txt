M64P Turnip is a Nintendo 64 emulator for Android. It is a fork of Mupen64Plus-AE (Mupen64Plus, Android Edition).

Features:
* Video plugins, including GLideN64 and the Vulkan renderer ParaLLEl-RDP.
* Turnip GPU drivers for the Vulkan renderer. You can import a driver zip file, or download one in the app from public projects on GitHub.
* RetroAchievements. It stays off until you turn it on and sign in.
* Save states, a cheat editor, and controller and touchscreen profiles.
* Netplay with other devices on your local network.
* Games in zip files, and in 7z files on Android 7.0 or newer.
* Color themes and presets.

The app needs Android 6.0 or newer. Turnip drivers need a 64-bit ARM device with an Adreno GPU and Android 9 or newer.

If a game has graphics glitches or runs slowly, try another video plugin. Some video plugins also have a frameskip setting.

The app shows no ads. It has no analytics and no crash reporting.
